Nina Sergeeva

Mosaic artist, monumental painting

Graduated with honours from the Faculty of Church Arts of St. Tikhon's Orthodox Theological Institute (PSTBI, now St. Tikhon's Orthodox University for the Humanities) in 2002 (diploma No. IVS 0092242), Department of Monumental Painting. For her first year and a half at the institute her principal teacher was Igor Lvovich Zelenetsky; her other teachers included I. Yu. Samolygo, N. V. Samolygo, L. G. Gacheva and S. V. Vasyutina, under the general direction of Evgeny Nikolaevich Maksimov.

Since graduating she has worked in monumental painting. She is the author, co-author or one of the makers of a number of works shown in the gallery. She designed the mosaic floor in the Church of the Saviour Not Made by Hands at the residence of the President of the Russian Federation and led the team that made it.

She also continues the teaching she began while still a student: she teaches fine art at a private gymnasium. Her total teaching experience (at school and in private practice) is 8 years.

Member of the International Association of Contemporary Mosaicists A.I.M.C.
