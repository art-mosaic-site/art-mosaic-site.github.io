Welcome to a site of an artist and mosaicist Nina Sergeeva. Here you may enjoy monumental and pictorial artworks of the master, purchase selected items or order custom mosaics. The author is a specialist in church art and designs interior for cathedrals or chapels. The site acknowledges readers with history of monumental art, specific mosaic and mural technologies. Here you may find useful relevant links and explore our online art-shop. The master tutors pupils in mosaics and fine arts.
